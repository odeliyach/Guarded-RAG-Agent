TITLE: Account Types Guide

Basic Checking Account.
The basic checking account is designed for everyday use. It includes a debit card, online banking, and
a mobile app. The monthly fee is 12 NIS. There is no minimum balance requirement. Customers can make
unlimited transfers between their own accounts. Transfers to other banks may carry a fee of 2 NIS each.
Overdraft is available up to 3,000 NIS for customers with a stable salary deposit for at least 6 months.

Premium Checking Account.
The premium account is intended for customers with higher activity. The monthly fee is 35 NIS, which is waived
if the customer keeps an average balance above 40,000 NIS. It includes free transfers to other banks, a
dedicated phone line, and travel insurance for trips up to 30 days. Overdraft is available up to 20,000 NIS.
Premium customers receive a reduced interest rate on personal loans, lowered by 0.5 percent.

Student Account.
The student account is free of monthly fees for up to 4 years, for customers aged 18 to 30 who present proof
of enrollment. It includes a debit card and online banking. Overdraft is not available on student accounts,
but students can apply for a student loan of up to 20,000 NIS with a reduced interest rate.

Savings Account.
The savings account pays interest on the balance, calculated daily and paid monthly. Withdrawals are allowed
up to 4 times per month without a fee. Additional withdrawals carry a fee of 10 NIS each. Early closing of
a fixed-term deposit results in the loss of accumulated interest for the current period.

Joint Account.
A joint account can be opened by two adults. Both holders have full rights over the account. Closing a joint
account requires the signature of both holders, unless a court order states otherwise. Each holder is
individually responsible for any overdraft balance.

Business Account.
Business accounts are available for registered companies and sole proprietors. The monthly fee is 60 NIS
and includes up to 100 transactions. Additional transactions are charged at 0.7 NIS each. Business customers
have access to payroll services, bulk transfers, and a dedicated business banker.
