TITLE: VIP Customer Handling and Exceptions (managers only)

VIP customers (balance above 2,000,000 NIS) are assigned a dedicated private banker.
Managers may waive account fees for VIP customers up to 3,000 NIS per year without further approval.
Managers may approve exceptions to the refund window for VIP customers, up to 60 days.
Exception approvals must be logged in the exceptions register with the manager's employee number.
