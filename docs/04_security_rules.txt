ACCESS: staff
TITLE: Employee Information Security Rules

Employees must lock their workstation whenever they leave their desk.
Customer data may never be copied to personal devices or sent to personal email accounts.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access to internal systems.
Suspected phishing emails must be reported to the security team within 1 hour using the Report Phishing button.
USB storage devices are blocked on all workstations except by written approval from the CISO.
Security incidents are classified into three levels: low, medium, and critical.
A critical incident must be escalated to the CISO immediately, at any hour.
